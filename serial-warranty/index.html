“Is my bike still under warranty?” One lookup answers it.

Type or scan the serial from the customer's email. The unit history shows the order it shipped on, who registered it, when the warranty ends and every claim about it, inside your Shopify admin.

Capture, register, claim. The same serial the whole way.

  1. At the packing bench

    Choose which products carry a serial and how many months of warranty each one gets. When you pack an order, scan or paste one serial per unit. A barcode scanner types the code and moves to the next line.

    The order saves only when every serial checks out. The serials are stored on the order as a custom field (a metafield named warranty.serials), ready for the packing slip.

  2. After delivery

    The customer registers on your store at /apps/warranty, a page that wears your theme. They enter the serial, their name, email and purchase date, plus up to 10 questions you write: short text, dropdown or yes/no.

    When the serial is on one of your orders, the warranty end is counted from the order date, not from what the customer typed.

  3. When something goes wrong

    The customer opens a claim at /apps/warranty/claim with the serial and their email, and follows it at /apps/warranty/status.

    You set the status, write a reply the customer sees and keep an internal note they don't. When email is on, they get a receipt and an update each time you reply.

It fits inside Shopify

Nothing to embed, no extra login for you or your customers.

Admin
Opens inside your Shopify admin, built with Shopify's own interface components. No separate login.
Storefront
Registration, claim and status pages open on your store's own domain, inside your theme's header and footer. (Shopify's app proxy serves them there.)
Orders
Serials are saved on the order itself, as a custom field (a metafield). Three lines pasted into your packing slip template (a Liquid snippet) print them.
Billing
Plans are charged through Shopify, on the same bill as your Shopify subscription.
Export
Download the registrations visible on your plan, with your custom answers, as a CSV file.
Data
When you uninstall, Shopify tells the app to delete your store's data, and it does.

Questions merchants ask

Something else? Email xthallis@gmail.com or read the support page.

Is the app on the Shopify App Store?

Not yet. Leave your email in the form on this page, or write to xthallis@gmail.com, and you'll get one email when it's listed.

Do my customers need an account?

No. They register and open claims with the serial number and their email address. The status page asks for both, so nobody can look up someone else's claim.

What if a customer registers a serial that isn't on any order?

By default it's accepted, and the warranty is counted from the purchase date the customer enters. If you only sell direct, turn on “Only accept serial numbers recorded on an order” in Settings.

Which barcode scanners work?

Any scanner that types like a keyboard. Each scan lands on its own line, and Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) saves the order. Camera scanning isn't built.

How far back can I capture serials?

Shopify shares the last 60 days of orders with apps, so capture serials when you pack.

Can I change the registration form myself?

Yes. In Settings you write up to 10 questions, each short text, a dropdown or yes/no, required or optional. No support ticket.

Which emails go out?

When the app's email is on (Settings shows whether it is): a confirmation when a customer registers, a receipt for each claim, an email when you reply or change a claim's status, and an alert to your store email for each new claim. Customer replies go to your store email.

What happens after 50 registrations on the free plan?

Registration 51 and later are still accepted, saved, and confirmed to the customer. In your admin their details stay masked until you upgrade to Pro, and then all of them unlock.

What doesn't it do yet?

Serial capture inside Shopify POS, camera scanning, photo uploads on claims, threaded conversations and bulk actions.
